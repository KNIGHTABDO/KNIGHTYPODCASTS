# Security Policy

Thank you for helping keep Knighty Podcasts secure. We take security issues seriously and appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose vulnerabilities.

## Supported Versions

Below is a table of current versions of Knighty Podcasts and their support status for security updates:

| Version | Supported          |
| ------- | ------------------ |
| < 1.0   | :x: (Not supported)|
| 1.0     | :white_check_mark: |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability, please do the following:

1. **Contact**: Email us at [knight007youtu@gmail.com] (or open a private issue if that is your preferred channel).
2. **Detail the Issue**: Provide as much information as possible about the vulnerability, including:
   - Steps to reproduce
   - Potential impact
   - Any suggested mitigations

We will review your report promptly and work with you to ensure a timely resolution of the issue. Please refrain from publicly disclosing the vulnerability until we have addressed it.

## Response Process

- **Confirmation**: We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 2-3 business days.
- **Investigation**: Our team will investigate and validate the vulnerability.
- **Fix & Disclosure**: Once confirmed, we will work on a fix or mitigation. After releasing a patch or update, we will credit you for the discovery (if desired) and publish details in our release notes or security advisories.

## Thank You

We sincerely appreciate your help in making Knighty Podcasts more secure. Your support and responsible disclosure enable us to protect our users and maintain trust in our platform.
